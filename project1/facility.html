Our Facilities

Home  |  Facility

Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University provides a wide range of academic and residential facilities to ensure a comfortable and enriching experience for every student on campus.

📚

Central Library

A well-stocked library with thousands of books, journals, and digital resources for research and academics.

🏠

Hostel

Separate residential hostels for boys and girls with mess, security, and 24x7 warden supervision.

🧪

Laboratories

Modern, well-equipped science, computer, and engineering labs for practical and research work.

🏟️

Sports Complex

Facilities for cricket, football, basketball, athletics, and indoor games to promote sports culture.

📶

Wi-Fi Campus

High-speed internet connectivity across the campus for students, faculty, and research scholars.

🚌

Transport

University bus service connecting the campus to Jaunpur city and nearby areas.

🏥

Health Centre

On-campus medical facility with a qualified doctor and first-aid support for students and staff.

🍽️

Canteen

Hygienic canteen and cafeteria offering affordable and quality food throughout the day.

🏦

Bank & ATM

On-campus bank branch and ATM facility for convenient banking access to students and staff.
